Community Day for Java 2026

Cancellation announcement

With a heavy heart, we announce the cancellation of Community Day for Java 2026 (24 Oct).

This was a difficult decision, and we’re deeply sorry for the disappointment.

To everyone who supported us, believed in us, and planned to join us, even from afar, thank you.

Your support means the world to us.

It is with a heavy heart that we share that Community Day for Java 2026, scheduled for 24 October 2026 at Gujarat University, Ahmedabad, has been cancelled.

The workshop planned for Saturday, 17 October 2026 is also cancelled, as it was part of this year's Community Day for Java initiative.

Despite our best efforts, the overall interest and response from the audience have been much lower than we anticipated.

A combination of factors contributed to this: the current uncertainty in the IT industry, changing dynamics in the age of AI, and university examinations coinciding with the event dates.

Given this response, we would not be able to deliver the experience we envisioned for our attendees, speakers and community partners. This was not an easy decision for our organizing team.

We understand that many of you were looking forward to these learning opportunities and may have already made plans to attend.

To our attendees, speakers, sponsors, review committee members, volunteers and community partners: thank you for your enthusiasm and trust.

We are genuinely sorry for the disappointment and inconvenience this decision may cause.

Please check your email for full details. Here is what happens to your ticket:

  • Refunds: We will initiate refunds for all purchased tickets through KonfHub.
  • No action required: You do not need to submit a separate refund request.
  • Timeline: Refunds generally take 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Need help? If your refund hasn't arrived by then, write to juggujarat@gmail.com with your registration details.

Although Community Day for Java 2026 and its workshop will not take place, our commitment to the Java community remains unchanged.

Gujarat JUG will keep bringing developers together through regular Java meetups, technical talks, hands-on workshops and other knowledge-sharing initiatives.

Thank you for believing in Gujarat JUG and being part of this journey. We hope to see you soon at one of our upcoming meetups.
